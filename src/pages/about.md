My name is Alp. I'm an undergraduate Computer Science student with an insatiable curiosity for breaking things—and more importantly, understanding how to fix them.

## The Mission

My primary focus is **Cybersecurity**, where I explore the dark corners of digital systems, hunt for vulnerabilities, and develop defensive strategies. I occasionally venture into other domains like Artificial Intelligence, Android Development, and Game Development, but security remains my true passion.

My ultimate goal is to become a **Security Researcher**, specializing in zero-day vulnerability discovery and responsible disclosure. Currently, I'm actively participating in bug bounty programs, sharpening my skills in real-world environments, and building the foundation for advanced exploit research.

## The Philosophy

> *"The best defense is understanding the offense."*

I believe that to truly secure systems, you must think like an attacker. This blog is my way of sharing that mindset—documenting vulnerabilities, explaining exploitation techniques, and discussing defensive measures that actually work.

Whether you're a fellow security researcher, a developer looking to secure your applications, or just someone curious about how digital systems can be compromised, you're welcome here.
